Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The 3AM Fridge Text"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-the-3am-fridge-text-4c3e721d): a program that writes the one line a smart fridge would text its owner at 3am.

## The entry

> 3am. Your 2am self hid the good snacks behind the kale. Your 3am self found them anyway. I watched. I kept the receipt.

119 characters, one line. The fridge as silent night witness — it watched the whole snack heist and kept the receipt.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22+) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, guarded to the competition's 1–140 character gate.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
